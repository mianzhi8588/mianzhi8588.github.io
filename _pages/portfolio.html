Research portfolio

From reliable sensing to embodied intelligence

My research connects signal recovery, mechanical-system diagnosis, multimodal perception, robot learning, and physical control. Across these projects, I focus on a common question: how can an intelligent physical system remain useful, interpretable, and safe when its sensors, models, or operating conditions are imperfect?

## Current research

The projects below are active research directions. Media are drawn from real prototypes, simulations, data-collection sessions, and exploratory analyses; unpublished implementation details and sensitive experimental settings are intentionally omitted.

Simulation environment

Robot-code reliability · Working paper

Hidden Safety Violations in LLM-Generated Robot Code

This project studies programs that complete an assigned robot task yet violate physical constraints. A MuJoCo-based evaluation layer checks collisions, joint and velocity limits, grasp stability, dropped objects, and restricted workspaces, then supports targeted repair before execution on hardware.

Research question: How should functional success and physical safety be evaluated separately for generated robot programs?

Dexterous manipulation · Working paper

LLM-Generated Hand Models & Reinforcement Learning

Prompt-guided generation and iterative refinement of CAD, URDF, and MJCF assets are combined with TD3 + HER training and drop-aware evaluation. The aim is to shorten model-building cycles while preserving physically meaningful joints, tendons, contacts, and task constraints.

Signal recovery · Preparing for submission

Inertial Sensor Overrange Recovery

A lightweight online framework detects short-duration overload, exploits cross-axis motion structure, adapts its observation window, and reconstructs clipped motion without future context. Evaluation uses public human-motion sequences alongside controlled MATLAB and self-recorded tests.

Multimodal manipulation · Working paper

Language-Conditioned Agentic Control for a 6-DOF Manipulator

This low-cost system translates natural-language goals into constrained perception and control primitives. The pipeline connects speech or text input, target grounding, pose estimation, inverse kinematics, action execution, visual verification, and recovery from common failures.

System emphasis: full-stack integration and recoverable task execution, rather than treating low cost as the research contribution by itself.

Wearable sensing · Early-stage research

Textile-Integrated Capacitive Sensing for Robot Control

A textile interface captures contact, deformation, and gesture-related capacitance changes, then maps calibrated signals to robot commands. Current work emphasizes repeatability, drift, latency, user variation, and robust command segmentation.

## How I approach a research system
01

Define failure modes

Separate task success from hidden failure: saturation, unsafe motion, poor transfer, unstable grasping, or ambiguous human input.

02

Build observable evidence

Use simulation, controlled experiments, public datasets, and physical prototypes to make system behavior measurable.

03

Design constrained methods

Combine learning with physical structure, geometry, safety checks, signal priors, or interpretable representations.

04

Evaluate the full loop

Report not only accuracy, but robustness, latency, generalization, failure recovery, and limitations.

## Selected earlier research
Condition monitoring · 2025

Wind Turbine Condition Monitoring and Bearing Fault Diagnosis

A PyTorch library and PyQt5 platform integrating SCADA and vibration signals, frequency-domain features, class-imbalance strategies, source-free deployment, and cross-machine evaluation.

Mechanical control · 2022–2023

Intelligent Vibration Reduction Support Design

Modeling and experimental validation of an active vibration-suppression platform using a voice-coil actuator, MATLAB/Simulink/Simscape, and LabVIEW control.

Functional materials · 2022

Radiative Cooling Films Prepared by Electrospinning

Material selection, electrospun film fabrication, and controlled thermal testing for passive cooling under sunlight exposure.

Multiphase dynamics · 2021

Dynamics of Droplet Impact on Thin Films

High-speed imaging and dimensionless analysis of spreading, crown formation, and morphology under varying impact heights and film thicknesses.
