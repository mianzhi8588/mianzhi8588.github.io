Research portfolio

From reliable sensing to embodied intelligence

My research connects signal recovery, mechanical-system diagnosis, multimodal perception, robot learning, and physical control. Across these projects, I focus on a common question: how can an intelligent physical system remain useful, interpretable, and safe when its sensors, models, or operating conditions are imperfect?

## How I approach a research system
01

Define failure modes

Separate task success from hidden failure: saturation, unsafe motion, poor transfer, unstable grasping, or ambiguous human input.

02

Build observable evidence

Use simulation, controlled experiments, public datasets, and physical prototypes to make system behavior measurable.

03

Design constrained methods

Combine learning with physical structure, geometry, safety checks, signal priors, or interpretable representations.

04

Evaluate the full loop

Report not only accuracy, but robustness, latency, generalization, failure recovery, and limitations.
