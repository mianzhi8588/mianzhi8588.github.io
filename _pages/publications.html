## Published
Published · 2025

IEEE Sensors Journal

Domain Adaptation With Joint Distribution Alignment Adversarial Learning for Open-Set Bearing Intelligent Fault Diagnosis

Yuanhao Geng, Gang Tang*, and Haoyang Wang

The study addresses bearing diagnosis when operating distributions shift and target samples may include previously unseen fault categories. Joint distribution alignment and adversarial adaptation are used to improve transfer while retaining the ability to recognize open-set conditions.
