## Published
Published · 2025

IEEE Sensors Journal

Domain Adaptation With Joint Distribution Alignment Adversarial Learning for Open-Set Bearing Intelligent Fault Diagnosis

Yuanhao Geng, Gang Tang*, and Haoyang Wang

IEEE Sensors Journal, 25(14), 26507–26519, 2025. The study addresses cross-domain bearing diagnosis when the target domain may contain previously unseen fault categories. It combines joint distribution alignment, adversarial adaptation, and open-set recognition to improve transfer under changing operating distributions.
