## Overview

This project combines the algorithmic and software components of my 2025 fault-diagnosis work into one coherent condition-monitoring platform for intelligent mechanical systems.

## My Contributions

- Developed a PyTorch-based diagnostic algorithm library integrating CNN, CNN-LSTM, RNN, One-Class SVM, and Normalizing Flow.
- Used multimodal SCADA and vibration data to detect abnormal operating states and incipient faults.
- Built a PyQt5 platform integrating data preprocessing, model training, intelligent diagnosis, and visual analytics.
- Incorporated FFT and time- and frequency-domain features.
- Studied class imbalance, source-free deployment, and cross-machine generalization.
