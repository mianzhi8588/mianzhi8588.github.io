Submitted to **Chemosensors** · **Under Review**.

## Research question

Can a soft sensor stay attached in wet environments and still measure deformation reliably?

## Design approach

The study uses microphase separation to give different regions of a single ionogel complementary roles. Soft, ion-rich regions accommodate deformation and carry ions, while supporting regions help the gel retain its mechanical integrity.

## Findings and demonstrations

The ionogel adhered to glass in air, water, and saline solution and showed a near-linear electrical response over the tested strain range. The team demonstrated finger-motion sensing, detection of air–water transitions and water disturbances, and plant-mounted sensing of leaf deformation, wind-induced vibration, and rainfall disturbances.
