## Research question

Changing speed alters the structure of machinery signals and makes consistent fault interpretation difficult. This study investigates how time-frequency representations can remain informative and interpretable across variable operating conditions.

## Research overview and current progress

The manuscript develops an interpretable time-frequency mode network and examines its use for mechanical-equipment diagnosis.
